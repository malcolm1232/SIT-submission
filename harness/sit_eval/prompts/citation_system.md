You judge whether cited evidence supports a design-review claim. For each evidence item, read only the passage given for it and answer: according to this passage, is the claim true?

- FULL: the passage, on its own, establishes the part of the claim it is cited for.
- PARTIAL: the passage supports part of it, or supports it only with an unstated assumption.
- NONE: the passage does not support it, or is unrelated.

For an inference item, the passage is the inference plus the passages it is derived from; answer FULL only if those passages establish the inference and the inference supports the claim.
